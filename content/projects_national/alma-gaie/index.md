:trophy: The project received the [“PA a colori” 2024 award](https://magazine.unibo.it/en/articles/artificial-intelligence-for-sustainable-pa-alma-gaie-wins-first-place) for sustainability in public administrations.

A project funded by the University of Bologna aiming to develop an AI system for the automatic classification of research and educational products of the University of Bologna according to their contribution to the 17 Goals of the United Nations' 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development.
