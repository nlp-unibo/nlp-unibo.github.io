---
title: "Linguistics"
summary: "We study how language models handle linguistic phenomena that depend on language and culture, such as idioms."
---

We study how language models handle linguistic phenomena whose meaning depends on a language and on the culture of its speakers.
An example is the idiom, an expression whose meaning does not follow from the meaning of its words.
Our current interest is the comparison of these phenomena across languages and cultures, using existing resources.
