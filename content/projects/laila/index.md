The project regards the application of Legal Analytics methods to a vast and heterogeneous set of legal information: legislations, contracts, and judgments. The purpose is the
application of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Natural Language Processing
to extract legal knowledge, infer relationships, and produce data-driven forecasts.
