From 2019 to 2021, the EU-funded AI4EU project built the first European AI On-Demand
Platform and Ecosystem to share resources, tools, knowledge, algorithms and more between
Member States. It aimed to increase innovation and technology transfer, accelerate the growth
of start-ups and SMEs, and fulfill the needs of the European AI community. The project
implemented eight pilots led by industrial partners to demonstrate the platform’s capabilities.
