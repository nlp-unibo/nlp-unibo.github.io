Elena obtained her Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Bologna.
She then completed her PhD in Computer Science and Engineering in the Language Technologies Lab, under the supervision of Paolo Torroni.
Her work focused on argument mining in decisions of the Court of Justice of the European Union on fiscal state aid, and on outcome prediction for Italian VAT decisions.
