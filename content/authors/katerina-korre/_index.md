Katerina Korre obtained her PhD from the University of Bologna, with a thesis on hate speech detection and, in particular, the problem of its definition, guideline construction, and data labeling.
Her current research interests include computational phraseology, pragmatics, and metaphor, as well as discussion moderation and facilitation.
