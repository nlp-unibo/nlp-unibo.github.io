Francesca Lagioia is Associate Professor in Legal Informatics, AI and Law, and Ethics for AI at the Department of Legal Studies of the University of Bologna, and holds a part-time professorship at the European University Institute in Florence.
Her research covers AI and law, privacy and data protection, consumer law, algorithmic fairness and explainability, and computable models of legal reasoning.
