Eleonora obtained her Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Bologna.
She completed her PhD in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Bologna on neurosymbolic AI and fairness, under the supervision of Michele Lombardi.
She then joined the Language Technologies Lab as a postdoctoral researcher, where she worked on guideline-centered data annotation.
