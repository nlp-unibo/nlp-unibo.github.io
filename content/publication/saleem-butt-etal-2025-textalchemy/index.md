abstract: 'Background and Objective: Literature reviews are becoming increasingly important for scientific research, but they
  are quite intense in terms of time and effort needed. Tools and techniques that can support scientists in these tasks exist,
  but they rarely focus on the data extraction. The present work introduces TextAlchemy, a system using off-the-shelf tools
  for the semi-automation of data extraction from scientific papers and tests its performance in the context of nutritional
  literature. Methods: The proposed pipeline applies retrieval augmented generation with advanced steps to include leveraging
  vision and optical character recognition models for document segmentation, context enrichment for optimal table data handling,
  query expansion for enhanced retrieval, re-ranking of the retrieved chunks for relevance, generation of insights with a
  large language model, and a vector database to enable for semantic associations. Moreover, a set of queries was designed
  to allow the extraction of specific information from 42 papers (involving 111 meta-analyses and 3108 extractions), taken
  from an umbrella review about the association between dietary habits and all-cause mortality. The data extracted were compared
  with the original extractions performed by human reviewers. Results: TextAlchemy achieved a precision of 92.93%, a recall
  of 90.01%, an F1-score of 91.91%, and a Work Saved over Sampling score of 78.51%. Among the results, 34 out of 42 articles
  and 20 out of 28 data extraction fields attained an F1-score greater than 90%, with some fields demonstrating perfect performance.
  Conclusions: The proposed pipeline showed promising performance, with quite a high level of agreement with the human extractors
  in the case study. If more evidence in this line is collected, the system could be reliably used to speed up the execution
  of literature reviews in the field of nutrition, helping to bring order to a research area where existing evidence is overwhelming
  and at times conflicting.'
